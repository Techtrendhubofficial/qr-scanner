Pavilion QR Pro

Google Play Store & PDPA / GDPR Compliant Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 | App Name: Pavilion QR Pro | Official Domain: qr.pavilionventure.com | Developer: Pavilion Venture

Core Privacy Guarantee (100% On-Device Processing): Pavilion QR Pro decodes QR codes, barcodes, and camera streams strictly inside volatile device memory using client-side WebAssembly / HTML5 Canvas. Zero images, video frames, or scanned documents are uploaded to remote servers.

1. Information Collection & Permissions

  • Camera Permission (android.permission.CAMERA): Used solely to provide real-time viewfinder frames to locate and decode QR codes and barcodes. We never record, stream, or store your camera feed.
  • Photo & Storage Access: When you choose to decode an image from your device photo album, the image is loaded strictly into local memory and processed on-device.
  • Internet Permission (android.permission.INTERNET): Used exclusively to load web assets, verify domain ownership via Digital Asset Links, and redirect to Stripe or Google Play Billing for subscription management.

2. Local Data Storage & User Ownership

All scan history, favorite items, and generated vector QR codes are stored locally on your device (in IndexedDB / Web Storage). You retain full ownership of your data and can export it to CSV, JSON, or TXT format at any time, or clear all history with a single tap.

3. Payment & Subscription Processing

Pavilion QR Pro offers monthly subscription tiers (Starter, Pro, Ultra) processed through official Google Play In-App Billing on Android and authorized Stripe Checkout for web access. All financial transactions are encrypted end-to-end (256-bit SSL). We never receive or store your payment card numbers.

4. Children's Privacy

Pavilion QR Pro does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. The app is a general-purpose utility tool designed for productivity and utility.

5. Third-Party Disclosures

We do not sell, trade, or transfer any user data to outside parties. No tracking SDKs or unauthorized third-party analytics compromise your on-device scanning security.
