Privacy Policy for Pavilion QR Pro

Effective Date: October 1, 2026 • Application: Pavilion QR Pro (Package: com.pavilionventure.qrpro)

Developer / Organization: Pavilion Venture

1. Overview & Commitment to Privacy

Pavilion Venture is committed to protecting your privacy. Pavilion QR Pro is a utility application designed for fast QR code scanning, barcode decoding, and QR generation. We operate under a strict Zero Data Retention (Zero-Log) principle. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal information or scanned data.

2. Camera Permission & On-Device Processing

Pavilion QR Pro requests permission to use your device's Camera (android.permission.CAMERA) solely for the following purpose:

  • Real-Time QR & Barcode Scanning: Camera frames are processed strictly in real time, locally on your device hardware, to decode QR and barcode data.
  • No Transmission: No video frames, photographs, or camera feeds are ever recorded, saved, uploaded, or transmitted to any external server or third-party cloud.

3. Photo & Storage Permission (Optional)

If you choose to scan a QR code from your photo gallery or save a generated QR image, the app will request temporary read/write access to user-selected media. This processing is performed 100% on-device.

4. Data Collection & Analytics

  • Personal Information: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or contacts through the scanner.
  • Scan History: All scanned records and generated QR codes are stored purely in your local device storage (Local Storage / Cache). You have complete control to view, export, or permanently delete your history at any time.
  • Third-Party Trackers: The application does not contain advertising trackers, background location trackers, or third-party behavioral profiling SDKs.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our application is not targeted at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be posted directly to this public URL: https://qr.pavilionventure.com/privacy.html.
